French and English option for the website. A simple and clear opening page with the trailer for the anthology, a short description of the screening, and live statistics on the number of screenings, partners, cities, karaoke songs, and cocktails drunk - the last two are fictional numbers

The statistics update every time someone is successfully accepted as a diffuser of the screening.

At the top is a menu with links to the 

programme page, 
Breakdown of each film including stills, directors, producers, country of production, and short synopsis
where to find a séance, 
Interactive live map of France that shows where and when séances can be found as diffusers sign up for them
press, 
Collection of press of everyone who’s covered the séance
Who we are 
Short description of Miyu distribution, who we are and links to our pages and YouTube channels, plus an easter egg link to a youtube/spotify playlist 
At the top right corner is a link to the portal for organisers where they can fill out a form with who they are. That we can then check and approve; once approved, they will automatically get a packet with further information. 

At the bottom is a link to a page with contact information for Miyu.
